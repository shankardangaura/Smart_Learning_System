Shapes and Their Areas

Circle

The area of a circle is calculated using the formula: A = πr², where r is the radius.

Square

The area of a square is calculated using the formula: A = s², where s is the length of a side.

Rectangle

The area of a rectangle is calculated using the formula: A = l × w, where l is the length and w is the width.

Triangle

The area of a triangle is calculated using the formula: A = ½ × b × h, where b is the base and h is the height.

Trapezoid

The area of a trapezoid is calculated using the formula: A = ½ × (b₁ + b₂) × h, where b₁ and b₂ are the lengths of the two bases and h is the height.
